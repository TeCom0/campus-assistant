### Arithmetic

The script wasn't changed. Each figure comes from the per-question ranks in the saved output, with
reciprocal rank = 1/rank, and 0 for a miss. Ranks are listed in the order of the evaluation set, with
the two removed questions left out.

| Method | Ranks of the 12 questions | Removed: Rhodes, McCall | Sum of reciprocal ranks, 14 to 12 | MRR on 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vector | 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 6, 2, -, 1, 7, -, - | 1, 2 | 8.3095 - 1 - 1/2 = 6.8095 | 6.8095 / 12 = 0.567 |
| Keyword | -, -, -, -, -, 1, 1, -, -, -, -, - | -, - | 2.0000 - 0 - 0 = 2.0000 | 2.0000 / 12 = 0.167 |
| Hybrid | 1, 1, 5, 1, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, -, -, - | 1, 1 | 9.2000 - 1 - 1 = 7.2000 | 7.2000 / 12 = 0.600 |
| Hybrid + English | 1, 1, 5, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 1, 3, -, - | 1, 1 | 10.0333 - 1 - 1 = 8.0333 | 8.0333 / 12 = 0.669 |

| Method | Hit@3: 14 to 12 | Hit@5: 14 to 12 | Hit@3 English: 9 to 7 questions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vector | 9 - 2 = 7 (Rhodes 1, McCall 2) | 9 - 2 = 7 | 7 - 2 = 5 |
| Keyword | 2 - 0 = 2 | 2 - 0 = 2 | 2 - 0 = 2 |
| Hybrid | 10 - 2 = 8 | 11 - 2 = 9 | 7 - 2 = 5 |
| Hybrid + English | 11 - 2 = 9 | 12 - 2 = 10 | 7 - 2 = 5 |

Hit@3 in French doesn't change, because both removed questions are English.
